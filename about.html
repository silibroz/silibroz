The idea

Silibroz started with one question — why does streetwear in India feel like an afterthought? We build brands that don't apologize for wanting more. Each label under Silibroz is independent in identity but united in standard.

The standard

We don't mass produce. We don't chase trends. Every brand in the Silibroz collective is built from the ground up with intention — the fabrics, the cuts, the design language. All of it deliberate.

What's next

Hermoza is the first. There will be more. Different categories, same obsession with quality and identity. Watch this space.
